# OnTrain - Personal Trainer Booking App

## What is OnTrain?
OnTrain is a mobile application that connects fitness enthusiasts with certified Personal Trainers (PTs). Users can browse trainer profiles, book training sessions, and manage their fitness schedules all in one place.

## Key Features

### 🎯 For Clients
- **Browse Trainers**: View a directory of available Personal Trainers with their specialties, contact information, and availability.
- **Book Sessions**: Easily schedule training sessions with your preferred trainer through an intuitive calendar interface.
- **Manage Schedule**: View upcoming and past sessions, cancel appointments, and track your fitness journey.
- **Specialty Search**: Find trainers based on specific fitness goals (Weight Loss, Bodybuilding, Yoga, etc.).
- **Profile Management**: Update your personal information and preferences.

### 👨‍🏫 For Trainers
- **Profile Visibility**: Showcase your specialties, certifications, and availability to potential clients.
- **Session Management**: View and manage bookings from clients.
- **Client Communication**: Direct contact information sharing for coordination.

## How It Works

### 1. Sign Up & Profile Creation
- Create an account as either a **Client** or **Personal Trainer**.
- Complete your profile with relevant details.

### 2. Discovery
- Browse the trainer directory or search by specialty.
- View trainer profiles with detailed information.

### 3. Booking
- Select an available time slot from the trainer's calendar.
- Confirm your booking with one tap.

### 4. Session Management
- View all your scheduled sessions in a calendar view.
- Receive reminders and manage cancellations.

## App Sections

### 🏠 Home Dashboard
- Personalized welcome with user greeting.
- Quick access to key features (Favorites, PTs, Gyms, Specialties, Records).
- Interactive calendar showing upcoming sessions.

### 👥 Trainer Directory
- Filterable list of Personal Trainers.
- Search by name or specialty.
- View detailed trainer profiles.

### 📅 Calendar View
- Weekly/monthly calendar display.
- Session markers for easy visualization.
- Tap to view session details.

### 👤 Profile Section
- Personal information management.
- Favorite trainers list.
- Payment and privacy settings.
- Help and support access.
